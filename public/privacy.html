Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

This policy covers the CheckMate mobile app (listed as “Chess Draughts Dominoes”, bundle id com.chessapp.chessApp). It describes what the app actually does with your data. It is not a legal guarantee of compliance with any particular regime; it is a factual description of our practices.

Short version: we collect what an account and a multiplayer game need. We do not sell your data and there are no advertising networks in the app. Your location is only shared if you switch it on yourself. You can delete everything from inside the app.

1. What we collect

Data Where it lives
Email address and username — required to create an account Firebase Authentication
Profile photo — optional, uploaded by you Firebase Storage
Chat messages — direct messages, group chat and in-game chat Cloud Firestore
Game data — moves, results, ratings, tournament and campaign progress Cloud Firestore and Firebase Realtime Database
Social data — friends, group membership, block list, privacy settings Cloud Firestore
Precise location — opt-in only, see section 2 Cloud Firestore
Notification device token — if you allow notifications Firebase Cloud Messaging and Cloud Firestore
App version and device model — attached to feedback and bug reports you send Cloud Firestore

We do not ask for your real name, postal address, phone number or payment details. The app has no in-app purchases.

2. Location and the player map

The app has a nearby players map. It is opt-in and off by default.

  • Nothing about your location is collected until you turn on Appear on Player Map in Settings → Privacy and grant the operating system location permission.
  • Once on, the app stores your precise coordinates and shows a pin for you on a public map. Any other person using the app can see that pin, along with your username and rating.
  • Your latest position overwrites the previous one. We do not keep a history of where you have been.
  • Turn Appear on Player Map off to stop sharing and remove your pin. You can also revoke location permission in your device settings, or use Invisible Mode to hide from other players entirely.

3. Notifications

If you allow notifications, Firebase Cloud Messaging issues a device token that we store so we can send game invites, move alerts, friend requests and message alerts to your device. You can turn notifications off in Settings → Notifications inside the app, or in your device’s own notification settings. Turning them off in the device settings stops delivery; deleting your account removes the stored token.

4. How we use it

  • To run your account, your games and your tournaments.
  • To show ratings, leaderboards, profiles and the player map.
  • To deliver the notifications you opted into.
  • To answer support requests, feedback and bug reports.
  • To detect cheating, harassment, spam and other conduct that breaks our Terms of Service.

We do not use your data to build advertising profiles, and we do not sell or rent personal information.

5. Third parties that receive data

Firebase (Google LLC) hosts authentication, the databases, file storage, messaging and our server functions. Everything in section 1 is processed by Firebase on our behalf, under Google’s privacy policy.

Three further services receive data directly from your device:

  • stockfish.online — when you play against a bot or run post-game analysis, the app sends the board position to this chess engine API over HTTPS. The service also sees your IP address, because your device makes the request. We do not send your username, email or account id with it.
  • tile.openstreetmap.org — the player map downloads map images from OpenStreetMap. That server sees your IP address and which part of the map you are looking at. Map data is © OpenStreetMap contributors.
  • fonts.gstatic.com (Google Fonts) — the app downloads its interface fonts from Google’s font servers, which see your IP address.

Each of those providers handles that data under its own privacy policy. We have no control over what they log.

6. What other players can see

Visible to other players: your username, avatar, ratings, game history, online status, group membership, and your map pin if you enabled it. Your email address is never shown publicly.

Settings → Privacy lets you restrict who can message, challenge or send friend requests to you, hide your online status, and hide yourself from the map.

7. Deleting your account

You can delete your account from inside the app:

Settings → Account → Delete account

Deleting removes your profile, username, email, profile photo, chat messages, friend and block lists, group membership, stored map location and notification tokens. Finished games may be kept in a form that no longer identifies you, so that your opponents’ own game records and ratings stay correct.

If you cannot reach the in-app option — for example you have lost access to the account — write to privacy@grandmasterapp.com from the email address on the account and we will delete it for you.

8. How long we keep things

  • Account and profile data — while the account exists.
  • Chat messages — until you delete them, or until the account is deleted.
  • Map coordinates — overwritten on each update, and removed when you leave the map or delete the account.
  • Feedback and bug reports — up to 24 months, so we can track recurring problems.
  • Backups — deleted data may persist in routine backups for up to 30 days before they roll over.

9. Security

Traffic between the app and our services is encrypted with TLS. Access to stored data is restricted by Firebase security rules, so one account cannot read another account’s private data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children’s privacy

CheckMate is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. The app contains public chat and a public map, which are not suitable for young children. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, write to privacy@grandmasterapp.com and we will remove the account and its data.

11. Your choices

  • See your data — Settings → Privacy → Request data download shows a copy of your account data that you can copy out.
  • Correct it — edit your profile in the app.
  • Limit it — Settings → Privacy controls visibility, messages, challenges, friend requests and the map.
  • Delete it — Settings → Account → Delete account.
  • Ask us — write to the address below for anything the app cannot do for you.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The current version always carries the “Last updated” date at the top of this page, and the same text is shown inside the app under About → Privacy policy.

13. Contact

Questions about this policy, your data, or a deletion request: privacy@grandmasterapp.com. We reply within 3 business days.
