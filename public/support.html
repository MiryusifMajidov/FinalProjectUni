privacy@grandmasterapp.com

We read every message and reply within 3 business days. Reports of harassment, impersonation or account takeover are looked at first, usually within 24 hours.

To help us find your account, include your username, the email address you signed up with, your device and the app version (shown in Settings → About).

Frequently asked questions

How do I delete my account?
  1. Open the app and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

This removes your profile, username, email, profile photo, chat messages, friend and block lists, group membership, stored map location and notification tokens. Finished games may be kept in a form that no longer identifies you, so your opponents’ records and ratings stay correct. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

If you have lost access to the account and cannot reach that screen, email us from the address on the account and we will delete it for you.

How do I report another player?
  1. Open that player’s profile — tap their name or avatar anywhere in the app — or open your chat with them.
  2. Tap the menu in the top right.
  3. Choose Report, pick a reason and send it.

Reports go straight to us with the username and the context. Report cheating, harassment, offensive usernames or avatars, spam and impersonation. Blocking the player as well stops any further contact immediately.

How do I block someone?
  1. Open the player’s profile or your chat with them.
  2. Tap the menu in the top right.
  3. Choose Block.

A blocked player cannot message you, challenge you or send you a friend request. To see or undo your blocks, go to Settings → Privacy → Block List and tap Unblock.

You can also cut down unwanted contact without blocking anyone: Settings → Privacy lets you limit messages, challenges and friend requests to friends only, or to nobody.

How do I turn off notifications?

In the app: Settings → Notifications, then switch off the categories you do not want — game invites, your turn to move, messages, friend requests and tournament alerts can be controlled separately.

To silence everything at the system level: iOS — Settings → Notifications → CheckMate → Allow Notifications off. Android — Settings → Apps → CheckMate → Notifications off.

How do I hide myself from the player map?

The map is off by default — you only appear on it if you switched it on yourself.

  1. Go to Settings → Privacy.
  2. Turn Appear on Player Map off.

Your pin disappears for everyone and the app stops sharing your location. You can also revoke the location permission in your device settings, or switch on Invisible Mode in the same screen to hide from other players everywhere in the app.

I forgot my password / cannot sign in

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password and enter your account email. A reset link arrives by email — check your spam folder if it does not appear within a few minutes. If the email address itself is no longer reachable, write to us and we will help you recover the account.

My game froze, or a move did not register

Check your connection first: moves are sent in real time, and a dropped connection can leave the board out of date. Close and reopen the app — games are stored on the server, so you will rejoin where you left off and your clock is corrected.

If it keeps happening, send a bug report from Settings → About → Report a bug. It attaches your app version and device model, which is what we need to reproduce it.

How is my data handled?

Our Privacy Policy lists everything the app collects, which third-party services receive data, how long we keep it and how to delete it. The same text is in the app under Settings → About → Privacy policy.

Still stuck?

Email privacy@grandmasterapp.com with your username and a description of what happened. Screenshots help.
